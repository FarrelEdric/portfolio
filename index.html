A short log from my job — six months of building, breaking and learning. One entry per month, written as I go.

01 / 06 written
  1. Month 01 AirNav Indonesia

    Development of a Carbon Emissions Dashboard

    Two things defined this month. First, the maths: I worked through the aircraft carbon-emission formula — how fuel burn, flight distance and flight frequency turn into a CO₂ figure, and what the assumptions behind it actually mean.

    Second, I turned that understanding into a working web application for calculating carbon emission, wiring the formula into something people can actually use. Alongside it, I started learning to work with the Hermes agent — picking up how to run tasks through it and where it genuinely speeds up the work.
